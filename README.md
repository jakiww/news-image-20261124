One image summing up the world's news of **Tuesday, 24 November 2026** — a newspaper front page, "THE WORLD IN ONE DAY", with six panels:

1. **Leak: 700,000 Russian war dead** — a Meduza/Mediazona investigation of leaked Rosstat death records puts registered Russian military deaths at 490,000 and the true toll above 700,000.
2. **11 dead in Kyiv region strike** — a Russian drone hits a five-storey apartment block in Dymer, north of Kyiv, in an overnight barrage of 458 drones and 45 missiles.
3. **24 dead in Indonesia factory fire** — a blaze at the PT Fonusa Agung Mulia confectionery factory in Cikande, Banten, kills 24 with around 30 still missing.
4. **Huang back as Nvidia CEO** — Nvidia's board votes unanimously to reinstate Jensen Huang as president and CEO, 11 days after his resignation; shares jump about 30%.
5. **Gaza a toxic wasteland** — an Oxfam and Conflict and Environment Observatory report finds indicators of potential contamination across more than 99% of Gaza.
6. **Youngest chess title match begins** — Game 1 of the World Championship in Geneva: defending champion Gukesh Dommaraju vs Javokhir Sindarov, both 20, the youngest title match in history.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

`main.ts` reads `entry.png` and writes it to the output directory. Run it twice and the bytes are identical.

The image is JPEG bytes under the name `entry.png` (allowed by the rules), 1024x1024 and under 1 MiB.
